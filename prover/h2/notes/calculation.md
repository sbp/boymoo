# Arithmetic search

Product normalization requests primitive and deduction Steps from
BookProof, and comparison calculation charges the BookProof rewriting
budget. Signed identities and total sums can request a negation
projection lemma through the derivation interface in
`prover/h2/sig/alist-recognizers.md`.

`h2-calculation-inapplicable-p` recognizes recoverable numeric evidence
and alignment failures. See [failures.md](failures.md).
