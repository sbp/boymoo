# Modulus recipes

The optional context lookup for `b1-modular-exact-remainder-integral` is
available through `b1-modular-exact-remainder-integral-with-claims`. The
shape-directed recipe supplies `:none` and the result of
`b1-search-fact-index-logic-claims` on the statement context's FactIndex.
Recipes with a specific equality name can use the three-argument call.
