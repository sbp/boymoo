# Simultaneous substitution

The simultaneous substitution proof joins the well-formedness Steps
for the translated term and its list of arguments. The theorem recipe
selects the requested conjunct of the preservation lemma.

`grep -nE 'b2-substitution-(substitution-preserves-term|preservation)' prover/b2/substitution.lisp` shows the proof operations. The command was run on 2026-11-23.
