# Floor ranges

The range proof relates a comparison of the quotient with a threshold
to the corresponding comparison of the ratio. An exact quotient is
derived from the lower and upper bounds of a unit interval. Equality at
the lower endpoint is part of that interval.

`grep -nE 'b2-floor-ranges-(floor-below|floor-point)' prover/b2/floor-ranges.lisp` shows the proof operations. The command was run on 2026-11-23.
