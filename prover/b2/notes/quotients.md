# Quotient measures

The floor bound recipe establishes naturality of the quotient, its
strict decrease, and a product bound. The descent recipe cites the bound
lemma when proving the recursive decrease for `ILOG`.

`grep -nE 'b2-quotients-(floor-bounds|floor-descent|ilog-decrease)' prover/b2/quotients.lisp` shows the proof operations. The command was run on 2026-11-23.
