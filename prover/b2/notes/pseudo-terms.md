# Translated terms

The pseudo-term recipes select their proof from the theorem name. They
use auxiliary recognizer and selector lemmas to establish properties of
term lists and alist lookups.

`grep -nE 'b2-pseudo-terms-(preserve-pseudo|alist-lookup|list-selector)' prover/b2/pseudo-terms.lisp` shows the proof operations. The command was run on 2026-11-23.
