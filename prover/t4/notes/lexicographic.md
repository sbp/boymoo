# Lexicographic measures

The field and parts proof operations
`t4-lexicographic-nat-list-fields-with-library` and
`t4-lexicographic-lex-parts-with-library` carry the
updated Library when they construct auxiliary lemmas. The measure
embedding converts a list of natural numbers to an ordinal. Its
entry points are in `prover/t4/sig/lexicographic.md`.
