# Rational order and signs

The ordered recipes share the arithmetic setup, indexed fact proofs and
constant computations of the tactic context. Their `-with-replies`
operations use the same reply table as the corresponding algebra
operations and preserve the Library across attempts.

Sign exclusion uses a proof of double negation, including evaluation
of comparisons on quoted rationals. The request forms are in
`prover/t4/sig/ordered.md`.
